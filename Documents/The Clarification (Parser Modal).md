# Zen Ledger - The Clarification (Parser Modal)

## Purpose
Resolves AI ambiguity without blocking flow.

## Layout
Bottom sheet modal (height: 40%).

## Key Elements
- **The Question:** "Is 'Apple' for..."
- **Option A:** Card: "Groceries? (Apple Store)"
- **Option B:** Card: "Tech? (Apple Inc)"
- **Option C:** "Something else..." (Opens category picker).
- **Original Text:** Displayed at top in quotes: "apple 14.99"

## Interactions
- **Tap Option:** Modal dismisses immediately, transaction saves.
